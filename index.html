02

Site plan

1:400 @ A3

Marjane sits to the east, so the car park is placed entirely on that side and the building screens it from the public road. One-way circulation: vehicles enter at the south gate, loop counter-clockwise through the two aisles and leave by the north gate. The gaming hall occupies the southern 37 m, the fast food and its terrace the northern 13 m.

03

Floor plan

1:150 @ A3

A single 7.00 m clear span with no internal columns, so the gaming hall stays completely free. The only fixed masonry core is the sanitary block at the junction, which serves both businesses and keeps all plumbing in one place.

Structure
Eleven galvanised steel portal frames at 5.00 m centres, 7.00 m clear span, IPE 160 rafters on HEA 140 posts. Isolated footings on a 15 cm reinforced slab.
Walls
60 mm polyurethane sandwich panel (Dalakit type) on the west, north and south faces, white micro-ribbed finish. 1.00 m blockwork base to take knocks.
East facade
Aluminium curtain wall, 6/12/6 double glazing with solar control, set back 0.60 m under the roof edge so the glass is shaded at midday.
Roof
Flat sandwich panel deck at 3% fall to a concealed gutter on the west side. Nothing is built on it — no terrace, no plant room, no second level.
Services
All wet points grouped in the sanitary block and the kitchen wall. Split air conditioning, with the condensers at ground level in the west service yard, out of sight from Marjane.
Signage
Two backlit boxes on the parapet, aligned with the two entrances, readable from the Marjane car park and from the fuel station approach.
Landscaping
Lawn only, on the perimeter strips, with drip irrigation. No water features, no planters, no decorative paving.
